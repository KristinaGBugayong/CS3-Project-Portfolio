## 2. Observe the Current Process

### 1. What process did you observe?

> *The process of replacing a broken or lost lab material consists of five different steps. Firstly, a form should be obtained from the lab registrar; after that, the student must then sign and give it to the teacher who supervises that specific lab activity when the material was lost or broken. Consequently, the teacher must sign the given form and return it to the lab registrar, the lab registrar will then sign it and give the student instructions and information about the material so he or she could replace it. Overall, the process will perform smoothly if the student, teacher, and lab registrar meet each other face-to-face.*

<br>

### 2. What is the purpose of this process?

> *The purpose of this process is to identify the broken item and to inform the necessary personnel. Without this process, there would be a lack of information on who broke the item, leading to the school losing valuable assets, having to spend their already limited budget on replacing old apparatus. Not only this, they also cannot reprimand the perpetrator due to them having a lack of evidence and knowledge of who did it, leading to them doing it over and over again, never learning from their mistake.*

<br>

### 3. Who are involved in the process?

**(Up to two roles only.)**

| Person/Role | Responsibility in the Process |
| ----------- | ----------------------------- |
| 1. Student         | Must report the situation and item lost or broken to the lab registrar. Sign the form given by the lab registrar, make the teacher handling the lab activity sign it, and replace the item.                              |
| 2. Lab Registrar         | Must record the item lost or broken and give the student a form for his or her to sign. Give the student information and instructions regarding the item lost or broken. Accept the replacement for the item.                              |

## 3. Analyze the Problem

### 1. What problems or difficulties did you observe?

> *The problems that I've observed are the process has too many steps and takes a long time, the student wait a lot just to get instructions or approval, many people need to sign the same form one by one and it makes it slow down, the student has to go different people each time which is tiring and slow, etc. *

<br>

### 2. Which part of the process could be improved or automated?

> *As observed, there are many parts of the process which could be improved or automated. These include reporting the broken laboratory item, receiving forms and physical signatures, and interpreting instructions. Many students often struggle to make teachers sign their forms as they still need to track down said teachers, which also makes the entire process very lengthy. By digitalizing these aspects of the process, the entire approval and notification process can happen easily and instantly online.*

<br>

## 4. Propose a Simple Solution

### 1. What would your proposed system do?

> *The proposal system acts as a replacement for the lengthy, traditional process of lab item replacement. The students fill out online forms to submit a report about the broken item, which can be directly sent to the teacher and registrar. When the report is approved by the higher authorities, then instructions can be sent to students detailing instructions on how to replace the item.*

<br>

### 2. Who would use the system?

| User/Role | Possible Use |
